# Competitive Analysis

Snapshot as of Oct 2026.

## The market
Paid voting for awards and pageants in Ghana is crowded. Players found include GGCL Events, OpenCastGH, RobSam Technology, FastVote, VoteMe.live, Easy Vote GH, BuddyVotes and BuiltFound. Nearly all lead with the same offer: **web voting, USSD voting and ticketing**. **None offer automatic nominee flyers.**

## Main benchmark: VotyHive (votyhive.smarthivelabs.com, Smart Hive Labs)
Built by a close friend of the founder. Clean interface and easy to navigate. Worth studying closely, ideally by talking to the builder about the stack and how the USSD aggregator is wired up.

What it offers:
- Branded organizer workspaces with subdomain, custom domain, colours and fonts
- Real-time results with organizer control over visibility (hidden, rankings, percentages, counts)
- Free and paid voting, per vote or in bundles
- Payment methods: MoMo, card and bank transfer
- Voting by web, "Vote by Code", WhatsApp, Telegram and USSD
- Event ticketing, public nominations, fraud protection, iframe embeds
- Built with Next.js (from the page code)

Pricing (Ghana, Oct 2026):
| Plan | Monthly | Platform fee on paid votes |
|---|---|---|
| Free | GH₵0 | 10% |
| Starter | GH₵27 | 10% |
| Growth | GH₵59.99 | 8% |
| Professional | GH₵100 | 7% |

## Where we can win at KNUST
1. **Auto-generated flyers.** Nobody offers this; it removes a real cost and delay.
2. **Campus focus.** Competitors pitch themselves as global or national. We are "the KNUST platform", with faculties, departments and halls in mind.
3. **In-person distribution** through the partner's existing relationships.
4. **Pricing that suits student groups:** commission only, 10% of gross, no subscription (decided 2026-10-14). Equal to VotyHive's free tier with none of its paid tiers.
5. **Shareable assets** sized for WhatsApp Status, where campus campaigns actually spread.

## Must-match features (baseline)
Organizer workspaces, web and USSD paid voting, MoMo, live results with visibility control, and payouts.
